==============================

person, company, email address (example.com) and phone number (+27 00) below is invented.

the HTTP webhooks, Bolt, the HubSpot and Anthropic SDKs, SQLite, retries and the dead letter replay.

(live 500 ms, 8 s, 6 attempts). Latencies are measured against local mocks, so real network and
model time are not included.
